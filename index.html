How are the two void-ratio series defined?

Initial-water-content basis (unshifted): initial water content determines dry mass from wet mass. Initial specimen volume, assumed particle specific gravity and subsequent measured volume changes determine the void ratio.

Final-water-content basis (shifted): a constant offset aligns the series with ef = wfGs, assuming full saturation. Water content is a mass fraction in this equation. The shift preserves volume-change increments and the compression slope; saved rounded reference values may leave a small rounding residual. The compression plot shows this shifted series.
